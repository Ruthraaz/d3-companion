What it does

In-game badge

A badge shows up next to the item tooltip: fits, fits for now (for leveling) or doesn't fit, with the affixes it has and the ones it's missing.

Maxroll builds

Pick the class and the guide, or paste the link of any Maxroll build. The app brings the items, skills, runes and paragon, and the hotkey starts using the build right away.

Plan your build

In the app (Planner tab) or right here on the site, nothing to install: items, gems, Kanai's Cube, skills with runes and paragon, with the game's data. Hover an item to see what it does. Open the planner.

My hero

Type your BattleTag: the app gets your hero's gear from Blizzard's official API and shows, piece by piece, what already matches the build.

Leveling and paragon

At which level each skill and rune of the build unlocks, and where to put your paragon points, in the game's order.

Mystic tip

Tells you which affix is worth rerolling at the Mystic to complete the item. If you already rerolled one, it knows only that one can be rerolled again.

Only looks at the screen

It reads the item tooltip the way you do: from the screen. It doesn't touch the game's memory or files.

How it works

  1. 1

    Download and install

    Open the installer, click Next and Install. The app shows up in the Start menu. The text reader (OCR) is included.

  2. 2

    Set up your builds

    Paste the link of a Maxroll guide (or pick one in the Guides tab). The build is imported and saved in the app, only the ones you use.

  3. 3

    Play and press F8

    With the game in windowed fullscreen, hover the item and press F8. The badge shows up next to the tooltip.

Icons and hero lookup (optional)

The icons, the Portuguese names and the "My hero" tab use Blizzard's official API, which needs a free key of your own. The LEIA-ME.txt in the app folder shows how to create one. Without the key, the badge and the builds work just the same.

Security and privacy

  • It doesn't read the game from the inside. No memory, DLL or DirectX: just a screenshot of the tooltip area when you press the hotkey.
  • Your screenshots stay on your PC and the app keeps only the 200 newest. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • It only talks to Maxroll and Blizzard, always over HTTPS. The app has no account, no data collection and no usage analytics.
  • Your Blizzard key is yours: it stays in a file on your PC (%APPDATA%\D3 Companion\.env), and the app window can't even read it.
  • Reviewed: size limits on everything that comes from the internet, links only from Maxroll, libraries checked against the known vulnerabilities database (OSV).

Planner account (optional): what is stored

  • It's only for saving builds in the cloud. Planning, copying the link and using the app still need no account at all.
  • You sign in with Discord or Google, through Supabase (the service that stores the builds). There is no password of ours.
  • What we store: your account number and the builds you save (name, class and the build). The builds table has no e-mail, name or picture. Supabase keeps with the account what Discord/Google send at sign-in (like the e-mail), only so you can sign in.
  • Nobody else sees your builds: the database only lets each account read and change its own.
  • Deleting is up to you: in the planner, "My builds" → Delete my builds (all of them) or Delete my account (the account and all its builds, right away). "Sign out" only disconnects this browser.

FAQ

Can I get banned for this?

The app only looks at the screen, like a video recording, and doesn't touch the game. Still, it's not made by Blizzard: use it at your own risk.

Does it work with the game in fullscreen?

Use Windowed Fullscreen in Diablo III's video options. In exclusive fullscreen, Windows doesn't let other programs draw on top.

Which language does the game need to be in?

English: the Maxroll builds and the tooltip reader use the English names. The app window can be in English or Portuguese (in Settings).

Do I need to install anything?

Just the app itself: the installer puts it in the Start menu, with no administrator needed, and it uninstalls from Windows Settings. Prefer no install? The release also has a zip. Your data stays in %APPDATA%\D3 Companion, so updating or uninstalling doesn't erase anything.

Can I use another key instead of F8?

Yes: in Settings, e.g. Ctrl+F8 or Alt+Q. If another program already uses the key, the app tells you which ones are free.
